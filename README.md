**Project Title: Brazilian E-Commerce Data Analysis**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset using Python and data analytics techniques to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, payment preferences, delivery operations, and product category performance.

The analysis combines multiple datasets through relational joins and applies exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI reporting, and data visualization to support business decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze revenue trends and marketplace performance
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate payment method preferences
* Measure logistics and delivery performance
* Analyze customer satisfaction through review scores
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Generate actionable business insights using data

---

## Dataset

Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset

The project integrates multiple datasets:

## Key Insights

### Customer Satisfaction

* Average review score: 4.03 / 5
* Majority of customers provided 5-star ratings
* Customer satisfaction is strongly associated with delivery performance

### Delivery Performance

* Orders were delivered significantly earlier than estimated on average
* Higher-rated orders were generally delivered earlier

### Payment Behavior

* Credit cards are the dominant payment method
* Customers frequently use installment-based payments

### Product Performance

* Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue
* Bed, Bath & Table generated the highest order volume
* Sports & Leisure and Computers & Accessories are among the leading categories

### Geographic Performance

* Revenue is concentrated in major Brazilian states
* Average order value varies significantly across regions
